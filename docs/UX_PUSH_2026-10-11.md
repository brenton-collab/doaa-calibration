## User-facing change

BOARD is now a chronological, time-first arrivals/departures workspace, sharing its model and filters with Airfield Movements. Date selection, previous/next day, Today/Live, direction and evidence filters, independent provenance disclosures and Inspector navigation form one connected journey. Phone/short-landscape controls start compact and expand deliberately; desktop/tablet controls start expanded. Reading state survives Flight/Contact investigation and returning to BOARD. Current associations remain qualified; unavailable dates do not imply zero traffic.

The shared renderer no longer creates synthetic aircraft to satisfy navigation. Published or explicitly established flights open Flight; observed associations open recorded Contact; unsupported inference opens provisional evidence. Airport inspection leaves Home/catchment unchanged. Built-in airport-local reference zones are explicit; other unknown zones remain labelled UTC.

# Product, interaction and Field Notes visual contract

## Purpose and audience
DOAA (David's Ottawa Aviation App) is an Ottawa-centred aviation **observation and investigation instrument**, not merely a live aircraft map. Primary user David; an authorized second user and future administrator-created users have private collections. The experience must be sophisticated, fast, quiet, information-rich and truthful about uncertain data.

## Canonical style — Field Notes Light
Default light theme: warm off-white, graphite, restrained greys, muted blue; matching Dark and optionally System. Contemporary macOS-clean aviation field journal, **not** faux paper, nostalgia, airplane silhouettes or generic dashboard cards. Use semantic tokens for surfaces, typography, borders, spacing, radii, state, altitude palette and focus. Clear editorial hierarchy; approximately 13–14px primary data and 12px secondary as starting points, tuned through real viewport review. Routine facts use unboxed datum rows and fine dividers; reserve soft rounded cards for meaningful clusters (telemetry, operational status, conflicts, media). Never clip critical facts or trade density for oversized empty cards.

Interaction grammar: ↗ only on actual cross-object navigation; ⓘ on independent field explanation/provenance; unmarked fact is inert. Whole navigable rows are isolated click/tap targets. No nested buttons, hover-only affordances, accidental scroll navigation, overlapping map and Inspector controls, or invented destinations. Keyboard, visible focus, screen readers, reduced motion, responsive touch targets (44×44 CSS px where practical), genuine loading/empty/stale/error states are release criteria.

## Surfaces
- **SKY:** plotting instrument with geographic depth, existing directional half-arrow aircraft symbology (never silhouettes), source-aware live/stale/degraded treatment, true bearing compass/range rings, trustworthy overlays only. Keep aircraft visually dominant. All identity-consistent observed trails remain visible for a rolling 60 minutes, age-faded, altitude-coloured when supported; persist after live marker disappears. Selection subtly emphasizes its trail without dimming others. Never draw invented movement across source/identity gaps. Visually suppress sub-pixel jitter only at render time. Follow selected aircraft north-up without changing zoom; deliberate pan pauses follow until explicit resume. Reduce marker motion on device request; snap only established discontinuities.
- **Rack:** readable, compact, scannable index of actual observations, not an arbitrary tile wall. Selection and source freshness agree with SKY and Inspector.
- **Inspector / Investigator:** shared persistent identity header, independently scrolling content and persistent navigation. On mobile, compact map-aware bottom sheet with **deliberate** expansion; on wide screens docked panel preserving usable SKY. Selection opens Contact Overview immediately with substantial stable telemetry, phase, freshness, evidence-backed route/status summary and Explore links. Keep cached data visible while enrichment arrives; no fake progress, stale response overwrites, or seven-field fallback masquerading as a complete dossier.
- **Flight:** locked contextual tabs **Overview · Timing · Progress · Journey** (Decision 176 supersedes earlier pending tab proposals). Rich carrier/operation identity, sourced routes and codeshares; punctuality and scheduled/estimated/actual times on Timing; observed position/phase on Progress; shared evidence-backed previous/current/next chronology on Journey.
- **Airframe:** **Overview · BIO · Operations · Media · Sightings**. Sighting is user-facing term for a DOAA electronic observation; internal canonical record remains Encounter. Never present external history as DOAA sightings. Media hero/gallery specificity EXACT/MODEL/TYPE only with supporting identity and immediate attribution; distinct historical/conflict badges.
- **Airport/Airfield:** locked contextual tabs **Overview · Operations · Movements · Airfield** (Decision 177 supersedes earlier Runways-tab proposal). Sourced profile, accurate METAR and timestamp, defensible advisory status, geographically accurate runway geometry/details, observed versus inferred use. No unsupported claim of normal operations or active runway.
- **BOARD:** time-first, dense, airport-local chronological arrival/departure register. Same canonical movements model as Airport Movements, with Published/Recorded/Inferred evidence, dates, bidirectional history where coverage exists, honest gaps and confidence. Distinct presentation, never separate contradictory engine. A route association is not proof of a current flight assignment.
- **LOOK:** explainable, balanced attention to interesting aircraft, unusual operations and locally visible events. Never manufacture rarity or causal claims. Dismissal does not rewrite objective significance.
- **AMBIENT:** required release-quality SKY and SLIDES compositions. Manual or safe idle entry; touch/interaction restores interactive SKY. User chooses composition, never auto-switch SKY ↔ SLIDES. SKY is quiet geography and trajectories. SLIDES prioritizes grounded live-airframe photos, then genuinely recently observed aircraft, with LIVE/LAST SEEN truth and time. Photo ~upper two-thirds blending into lower information field; aviation facts lead, attribution accessible. Rotate on meaningful observed events, not arbitrary chatter. Respect reduced motion and burn-in.
- **Search and history:** real identity- and time-aware results, no fabricated associations or counts. Historical result opens a time-correct Inspector preview **without** silently changing live map; explicit transition to historical context and Return to Live Workspace preserve navigation, selection, map, filters and provenance.

## Evidence-driven visual states
Distinguish live, stale, last observed, interrupted feed, restored feed, degraded position, conflicting sources, inferred movement, historical context, pending and unavailable. Map warning treatment stays restrained; substantive source comparison belongs in Inspector. No data is not zero traffic; unknown is not zero; no advisory is not Normal.

## Epistemological confidence in the experience (2026-10-12 clarification)

**Assert what the evidence warrants. Qualify only what materially changes the interpretation. Make the reasoning available without making it compulsory.**

DOAA is an aviation understanding instrument, not a provenance dashboard. Observations, published records, retrieval, derivation and reasoned inference may combine into confident conclusions. Present the best defensible synthesis directly when evidence supports it. The interface should absorb the complexity of source reconciliation and expose its reasoning through optional inspection, not mandatory badges, source prefixes, confidence scores or ubiquitous caveats.

Visible qualification is a *materiality decision*, not a source-type rule. Qualify genuinely ambiguous identity, time, operational status, route association, location or contradictory evidence when omission would change a reasonable interpretation. Do not automatically prepend “likely” to a well-supported inference or “derived” to every calculation. Do not mask important uncertainty for visual polish. The internal evidence contract still requires provenance, freshness, validity intervals, competing claims and auditability.

This clarification supersedes any interpretation of prior “Evidence-driven visual states,” evidence badges, and inferred-value qualifier examples as a mandate for constant visible labeling. Preserve meaningful live-versus-last-seen distinctions and genuine degraded states, but choose their expression through actual UX exploration. Neither the inherited Inspector dock/sheet nor the current Rack and tab layout dictates future spatial design. Investigation **origin**, **focus**, and **workspace** are separate concepts; the relational Inspector is an interaction model, not a required panel.

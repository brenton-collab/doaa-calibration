# SKY map UX checkpoint — 2026-10-11

## Delivered scope

A compact MAP entry sits beside provider usage. Its keyboard-accessible modal separates Mode, Base style and Layers. TRAFFIC and BASE/STREET operate; MET and NAV are disabled with coverage explanations. Base changes preserve camera and selection. The modal closes by its button or Escape and returns focus. This implements the control structure of Decision 64, not the absent NAV/MET datasets or workspaces (56–63, 65).

Selecting a fresh, non-ground, six-hex aircraft observation starts following. A deliberate pan, pinch or wheel zoom pauses it while retaining selection and Inspector. FOLLOWING can pause; RESUME FOLLOW explicitly resumes. Fresh positions reposition the camera without changing zoom or north-up orientation. Supported reported track provides a modest forward view; missing direction centers the aircraft. Layout calculations account for header, Rack and compact/docked Inspector. Stale, pinned, future-dated or unlocated observations cannot move the camera; the control says LAST OBSERVED and is disabled. No predicted position, marker interpolation or animation is added. Follow operates on the existing feed's aircraft identity contract; it does not complete the more demanding cross-source identity/conflict reconciliation requirements. Thus Decisions 119–123 remain partial.

Every available session trail is rendered independently of its marker, selection or current feed availability. Samples expire against real elapsed time after one hour; selected trails gain modest opacity/width without dimming the others. A local redraw every ten seconds ages trails even during interruptions; it makes no network request. Previously loaded samples survive empty/failed acquisition. Invalid coordinates, future timestamps and expired samples are excluded; same-timestamp source updates replace prior samples. Source identity boundaries remain the upstream contract. No trail geometry is simplified, hidden by density or made clickable. The all-trails checkbox changes display only, and resets to visible on reload without account/local storage writes.

The fixed palette maps 0, 10,000, 25,000 and 45,000 feet to pale blue through navy. Values outside the range use endpoint colours; unknown altitude is neutral grey. Segments blend each endpoint's colour, including blue-to-grey, as a visual transition rather than intermediate measurements. The scale and limitations live in map settings; units change its labels, not calibration. Aircraft details retain numeric altitude information. Barometric altitude is preferred over geometric when explicitly available; legacy track payloads contain only `alt`, so their measurement basis remains unspecified. No claim of complete per-point altitude-quality/provenance is made. Connectors represent reported sequence, including jumps, not verified flown paths. The map trail extent follows Decision 128's one-hour supersession, not Decision 125's earlier full-flight scope.

## Verification and limits

152 Node tests cover the fixed palette, zero/unknown values, measurement precedence, bounds, aging, retained/merged history, invalid samples and fresh follow eligibility/targeting, along with existing regression coverage. `scripts/sky-browser-acceptance.cjs` exercises map controls, camera preservation, default follow, pan/wheel pause, explicit resume, retained selection/zoom, session visibility reset and responsive containment at desktop, phone, short landscape and tablet sizes. Final full browser and dense movement regressions each passed all four sizes; the SKY suite also passed a final repeated run. All report frontend hashes match the checkpoint. Reports are preserved in docs/qa. Offline screenshots label their fixture data; they do not verify geographic provider coverage, authenticated Preview or physical devices.

Unfinished: NAV/MET sources and layers, weather time semantics, unified search, cross-device recent-trail restoration, per-point source/measurement quality, identity/conflict reconciliation, smooth marker animation and discontinuity policy, comprehensive stale-marker/outage handling and live/provider acceptance. Matrix rows remain partial where these requirements or deployment verification remain open. No full release acceptance is implied.

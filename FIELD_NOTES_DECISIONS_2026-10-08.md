# DOAA Field Notes: canonical product decisions (2026-10-08)

Status: design decisions and reported defects, not implemented features. Read alongside DOAA_SPEC.md and current code.

## Identity and map
Field Notes Light is the intended default. Offer matching Dark for nighttime, optionally System. Shared semantic design tokens, browser-local preference. Restrained macOS-clean aviation field-journal feel: warm off-white, graphite, greys, muted blue, readable typography, subtle dividers. No faux paper or nostalgia. Sky is a plotting chart; Rack an observation index; Investigator a dossier; BOARD a movement register.

Aeronautical chart conventions are inspiration, not a request to reproduce VNC charts. Preserve the carefully tuned existing map character, geographic detail, depth and composition. Recalibrate map colours, contrast, opacity and perceived weight for light theme as needed; compare identical zoom and location before/after. Keep existing aircraft directional half-arrow markers and track geometry/behaviour, rendered dark rather than white in light mode; do not use airplane silhouettes.

Explore graduated blue distance rings integrated with a bearing compass on the outermost visible ring, rather than a floating compass. Example spacing at appropriate scale: 25/50/75/100 NM, adapted to catchment and zoom. Mark true north and true bearings. Consider sourced, authentic controlled-airspace boundaries in muted red/magenta, with meaningful labels and limits, not invented shapes. Aircraft remain dominant; overlay visibility may be selectable.

## Units
Provide a global display preference accessible from catchment/settings: NM with knots, km with km/h, mi with mph; altitude feet or metres independently. Keep GS label with selected speed unit. Apply across Sky, Rack, BOARD, search, Investigator, history and ring labels. Canonical stored measurements and physical catchment/ring geometry must not change on unit selection; convert at render time. Preserve flight levels, runway designations and other aviation conventions where necessary.

## Investigator and interaction
Navigable datum uses a muted ↗ arrow; explanation uses ⓘ; plain data has neither. Whole navigable row is clickable, but hit areas must be isolated from adjacent rows and independent ⓘ controls. Do not nest buttons. Support keyboard focus, clear pressed feedback, touch-friendly targets and no accidental navigation during scrolling. Keep label and ⓘ together on wrapping. Increase overly tiny typography across Rack, BOARD, search and Investigator; roughly 13–14px primary data and 12px secondary as starting points.

Close button must never obscure data: reserved persistent header area, independently scrollable body and persistent bottom navigation tray; validate phone portrait, short landscape, tablet and desktop.

Contact answers what is here now; Flight describes the current operation; Aircraft gives a full sourced physical-airframe biography; Journey gives verified operational sequence; Media covers visual identity; Encounters lists only actual DOAA observations. Relationship rows should name the relationship and offer ↗ drilldown where available. Normalize raw strings, dates and repeated records into legible rows/timelines. Hero photograph shows grounded EXACT, MODEL or TYPE specificity pill (or none if unknown). Expand Media beyond a single photo where licensing and evidence permit, with attribution, dates, livery and specificity. Inventory available live, D1 and external data before deciding surface ownership and density.

## Correctness defects and future work
Journey BEFORE/NOW/NEXT means immediately previous/current/immediately next actual airframe operation, not consecutive D1 records or callsign matches. NOW must agree with Flight. If adjacency is not evidenced, say previous flight not established or next assignment unknown. Keep observation chronology separate from operational assignment sequence.

BOARD reportedly shows no arrivals/departures or incorrect ARR/DEP. Diagnose source coverage, airport-centred airborne YOW movement acquisition, statuses and classification. Differentiate no data from no traffic. BOARD must remain independent of Sky's spatial catchment.

Develop rich airframe biography with manufacturer, model, age, MSN/serial, engines, registration and operator history, liveries, sourced photos and genuine DOAA Encounters, with epistemic qualifications. Aviation-facing answer first.

Replay must distinguish DOAA Observed Replay from timestamped D1 Samples, with no future knowledge leakage, from External Historical Reconstruction for missed flights. Search and external history must never manufacture DOAA Encounters.

Validate the merged explainer-wiring change and any late PR review feedback in the actual browser. CI green alone is not UX validation.

## Implementation guardrails
Separate data correctness repairs from visual treatment where practical. Prefer shared tokens and reusable datum interaction components, reversible map treatment and real-device comparison. Preserve the existing Encounter invariant and update the main handoff to point here when repository writes permit.

## Locked design review decisions (2026-10-10)
- **Inspector viewport: HYBRID (locked).** On mobile, use a map-aware compact bottom sheet by default with deliberate expansion into a larger investigation workspace. Keep SKY visible in compact state; expansion must not happen automatically on selection. On wider screens use a docked panel that preserves a usable SKY viewport. Reserve independent header/close and navigation regions, scroll the content separately, and prevent BASE/STREET map controls from overlapping Inspector controls. Validate on actual phone portrait/landscape, tablet, and desktop. This is a design decision, **not an assertion that current UI implements it**.

- **Visual language: C — Hybrid editorial/modular (locked 2026-10-10).** Retain Field Notes Light: editorial headings, quiet dividers and readable unboxed datum rows for routine facts; soft rounded cards for meaningful groups such as telemetry, operational status, evidence conflicts and media. Standardize semantic surfaces, spacing, radii, pill treatment, typography and wrapping across SKY, BOARD and Inspector. Do not turn every field into a card. This is design intent, not a claim of implementation.

- **Inspector tabs: shared core plus contextual tabs (locked 2026-10-10).** Use a stable Inspector shell, persistent object identity/header and consistent core navigation, augmented by object-specific tabs for Contact, Flight, Airframe, Airfield and other supported objects. BIO, Journey, Media and Encounters are not automatically all top-level object types; place them according to their semantics and evidence. Cross-object links preserve navigation history/back behaviour. Exact core-tab names and per-object inventory remain to be decided; do not infer that the earlier illustrative tab matrix was approved wholesale. Design decision only, not yet implemented.

- **Data presentation and normalization (locked 2026-10-10).** Preserve raw source values and provenance in D1; separately normalize known identities/relationships only with evidence and format values at the presentation boundary. Human-readable names and descriptions use appropriate case; preserve ICAO codes, registrations, callsigns and acronyms. Break concatenated concepts into legible rows/lines only when the underlying relationships are supported; allow natural wrapping and paragraphs for biographies and provenance. Distinguish unknown, unavailable, conflicting and not applicable. Standardize units, dates, coordinates and numbers across SKY, BOARD and Inspector without changing canonical measurements. Important information must not be clipped or ellipsized. These are locked design requirements, not yet fully implemented.

- **Interaction grammar and accessibility (locked 2026-10-10).** A muted ↗ marks a real cross-object navigation target; the whole isolated datum row activates it and preserves Inspector back history. An independent ⓘ explains the field, provenance, confidence or interpretation in context, without navigating. Unmarked facts imply no interaction. Keep labels and their ⓘ together across wraps; never nest buttons or allow overlapping hit areas. Provide keyboard access, visible focus, subtle hover/pressed feedback, touch targets at least 44×44 CSS px where practical, and no hover-only/long-press-only affordances. Do not display ↗ without an actual destination. Implement and test consistently in SKY, BOARD, search and Inspector. Locked design requirements, not implementation verification.

- **Media, attribution and evidence pills (locked 2026-10-10).** Media hero image only when grounded; show EXACT only for evidenced same physical airframe (registration/ICAO24 with adequate matching evidence), MODEL for evidenced same confirmed model, TYPE for broader evidenced family/type. Omit specificity pill where not justified; never suggest unrelated imagery matches. Separate photographic specificity (EXACT/MODEL/TYPE) from evidence-state pills (HISTORICAL/CONFLICTING), which may coexist. Display photographer/source attribution immediately below image, capture date, livery and licensing where known, and provide expandable Media gallery for additional grounded imagery. Place small pills legibly without obscuring subject or controls. These are locked design requirements, not claims of completed ingestion, licensing or implementation.

- **Inspector universal navigation: Overview only (locked 2026-10-10).** Every inspectable object has Overview plus only its relevant contextual tabs; there is no universal Evidence or Relationships tab. Relationship links appear alongside relevant facts. Evidence stays attached to each datum through ⓘ provenance/explanation, with visible CONFLICT/HISTORICAL/INFERRED pills only where consequential; an optional discreet record-level source-summary pill opens an on-demand evidence ledger. A source count is not a confidence score, and repeated upstream evidence is not independent corroboration. Exact contextual tab names and field inventories remain open for review. Locked design intent; not yet verified in deployed UI.

- **Airframe contextual tabs and terminology (locked 2026-10-10).** Airframe tabs: Overview, BIO, Operations, Media, Sightings. Keep Operations (evidenced operational history) distinct from Sightings (events actually observed by DOAA). **Sightings** is the user-facing label; **Encounter** remains the internal canonical observation record type. Explain via ⓘ that a DOAA Sighting is an electronic observation, not necessarily a human visual sighting. External searches, inferred history or replay must not create Encounters. Exact field-to-tab mapping remains subject to source inventory.

- **Flight Inspector refinement (design direction, pending final tab lock; 2026-10-10).** Route header should show a thin progress track and aircraft-position dot/glyph when trustworthy endpoints and position exist; geographic progress must not masquerade as time elapsed, stale positions must be timestamped, and unknown progress must not be invented. Overview includes a prominent Timing section separating scheduled, estimated, actual (off-block/takeoff/landing/on-block where evidenced), elapsed/remaining and freshness. Progress includes a compact geographically grounded route/position map with aircraft glyph and a route to SKY, plus honest stale/missing states. Journey must be a *timestamped operational chronology*: each independently verified prior/current/next operation has its own departure/arrival time evidence and time basis, route, identifiers and airframe association, with gaps visible and no adjacency-based invented operations. This is agreed direction; exact Flight tab set still pending confirmation.

- **Flight Inspector additional requirements (design direction, pending Flight tab lock; 2026-10-10).** Timing means *punctuality*: show scheduled departure, actual departure, scheduled arrival, actual arrival in a stable four-field layout, and early/on-time/delayed difference when supported; distinguish estimated times from actual and specify departure versus arrival delay. Progress includes inferred/observed flight phase (ground, climbing, cruising, descending etc only at defensible precision), current altitude/flight level, groundspeed, vertical rate, track/heading, squawk, position, freshness and history-supported highest/lowest altitude and maximum speed; reuse coherent telemetry presentation with Contact rather than conflicting duplicate interpretations. Across Inspector, expected fields remain visible and clearly distinguish UNKNOWN, NOT AVAILABLE, PENDING, CONFLICTING and NOT APPLICABLE. Inspector opens promptly with cached/known facts while slower enrichment runs; show real loading activity, elapsed time and per-source/field pending states, incremental updates, explicit timeout/failure and stale data, without fake percentage progress; prevent stale responses overwriting newer object selection. Investigate the reported up-to-30-second Inspector enrichment latency during implementation. These are design requirements, not claims of implemented functionality.

- **Contact flight phase and interesting behaviours (design direction, 2026-10-10).** Distinguish baseline movement phase (ground/taxi, takeoff, climb, cruise, descent, approach/final, landing, unknown) from concurrently detected manoeuvres/events (holding pattern, circling, go-around, diversion, touch-and-go, loitering and other defensible special activity). Display phase and notable behaviour separately, e.g. Descending + Possible holding; do not force mutually exclusive classifications. Derive from actual time-ordered telemetry/track and airport/runway context where available, with explicit confidence, observation window and timestamp. Never infer ATC instructions, pilot intent, actual touchdown or destination change solely from an ambiguous ADS-B trace. Consider timestamped detected events in Contact Observations; no claim these detectors currently exist. Flight/Contact tab architecture still under review.

- **SKY first-click Contact composition and Flight ownership (approved 2026-10-10).** Clicking a SKY contact/track opens Contact Overview immediately in the shared Inspector shell. Present callsign/identity, current phase and freshness, a prominent compact origin→destination progress strip and punctuality summary only when evidenced by a linked Flight, immediate contextual access to Flight/Airframe/Airfield and shared Journey, a substantial stable telemetry grid (including altitude types, flight level where justified, groundspeed, vertical rate, track, squawk, on-ground, position age; unavailable values explicitly labelled), and recent notable activity. Flight owns detailed scheduled/estimated/actual departure and arrival timing, early/on-time/delay analysis, geographic route mini-map and operation details; Contact does not duplicate those full sections. Journey is a single shared evidence-based timestamped chronology reachable from Contact and Flight. Track History concerns within-session telemetry/events, distinct from Airframe Sightings (separate observed encounters). Keep route and progress absent/unknown rather than inferred when ungrounded. This locks first-click composition and responsibility boundaries; precise Contact tab naming and final navigation mechanics remain open.

- **Journey presentation (locked 2026-10-10).** One shared Journey view accessible from Contact and Flight: a prominent previous/current/next operation summary followed by a detailed timestamped chronology of additional evidenced operations for the same airframe. Each operation distinguishes scheduled, estimated, observed and actual times, route, identifier, evidence/provenance and verification status; show dates and timezone, including across midnight. Distinguish next *scheduled assignment* from next *observed operation*; do not conflate either with database adjacency. Unknown previous/next remains explicitly UNKNOWN, with gaps and uncertain airframe associations visible. No invented operations or continuity. Locked presentation; implementation and data coverage remain unverified.

- **Flight identity richness and Explore consistency (design refinement, pending final Flight lock; 2026-10-10).** Flight Overview should be worth investigating, not merely show route/times: full evidenced marketing and operating carrier names and IATA/ICAO identifiers, appropriately sourced/licensed operator logo, codeshare flight numbers, airline group/alliance affiliation when verified, and clear differentiation between marketing carrier, actual operator and alliance. Codeshares can expand without crowding header; unknown relationships remain explicit. Preserve substantial punctuality Timing, route progress and geographically grounded mini-map. Reuse the shared **Explore** label and contextual cross-object navigation cards/links established on Contact for Flight, Airframe, Journey, origin/destination Airfields and other real associations, with back history. Do not display unverified logos, codeshares or affiliations as fact. Design direction only; Flight tabs remain unfinalized.

- **Flight Inspector composition and tabs (locked 2026-10-10).** Flight has Overview and shared Journey. Overview is a substantial but restrained investigative page: route header with grounded progress line/dot; rich carrier identity including full marketing and operating names, identifiers and licensed logos where available; alliance/group affiliation and expandable codeshares directly on Overview (not a sparse extra tab); punctuality-oriented Timing with scheduled versus actual departure and arrival, clear early/on-time/late deltas and distinct estimates; geographically grounded route mini-map and aircraft glyph; and a consistently named **Explore** area linking to associated Contact, Airframe, Journey, origin/destination Airfields and other established records. Flight owns these operational/schedule details, while Contact owns detailed live telemetry and phase. Missing fields remain visible with accurate state labels; no fabricated affiliations, routes, times or logos. Journey is the same shared timestamped chronology from Contact. Locked design, not yet implemented/verified.

- **Airfield Overview operational status and METAR (design refinement, 2026-10-10).** Add a prominent clickable airport operational-status pill in the Airfield header when grounded (e.g. ground stop, ground-delay programme, arrival/departure restrictions, runway closure, diversion activity, significant disruption). Its contextual detail reveals actual source/authority, advisory text or interpretation, affected traffic, effective period and freshness; support multiple concurrent advisories. Do not equate lack of an alert with Normal; use No verified advisory/UNKNOWN as appropriate, and do not label airport-wide diversion activity from a single flight without evidence. Display raw METAR faithfully with observation timestamp and separate retrieval freshness; clicking METAR or adjacent ⓘ opens a plain-English breakdown (wind, visibility, clouds/ceiling, precipitation, temperature/dew point, altimeter, flight category when supported). Weather is not itself proof of an operational restriction. TAF, if later added, is clearly a forecast. Source coverage and live availability require implementation audit; no invented operational states.

- **Airfield hero and runway interaction (approved 2026-10-10).** Airfield Overview leads with a sourced, attributable airport photograph and a substantial airport profile: identity, codes, operator, geography, elevation, timezone, classification, and available operational facts. Preserve airport advisory status pill with source details, raw METAR with plain-English explanation, airport activity statistics, and consistent Explore links. Replace the generic mini-map with an interactive, geographically accurate runway layout based on verified runway endpoint coordinates. Selecting a runway shows an inline details panel for designation, length, width, surface, lighting, approach aids and evidenced restrictions/status; unknown fields remain visible. Include accessible labelled runway selectors for keyboard and touch. Do not treat a schematic as verified geometry or infer official runway use from sparse tracks. Overview, Runways and Movements remain proposed tabs; data coverage and implementation are unverified.

- **Airfield tabs (locked 2026-10-10).** Airfield Inspector uses Overview, Runways, and Movements. Overview retains the sourced photographic hero, comprehensive airport profile, clickable operational status, raw/decoded METAR, interactive geographically grounded runway layout with inline selected-runway details, activity summary, and consistent Explore navigation. Runways provides deeper specifications and evidenced operational restrictions; Movements separates scheduled/published movements from DOAA-observed traffic. Preserve UNKNOWN/NOT AVAILABLE and source freshness. Design lock only, not implementation verification.

- **Movements and BOARD unified architecture (locked design direction 2026-10-10).** Airfield > Movements is a time-navigable airport activity timeline: browse backward through recorded movements, inspect current activity, and browse forward through genuinely sourced scheduled operations. Direction and evidence-type filters; clear local airport dates/times, provenance, completeness/freshness and gaps. BOARD is a second, roomier presentation/entry point of the same canonical Movements model, not a duplicate movement engine or competing data product. Shared filtering, matching, punctuality, status and Flight/Contact navigation preserve Inspector back history. No synthetic historical movements or invented future schedules; source availability governs each temporal region. Exact time-navigation interaction and BOARD presentation remain to be refined.

- **Movements time navigation and evidence classes (locked 2026-10-10).** Both Airfield Movements and BOARD use the same canonical, airport-local chronological movement timeline with three complementary navigation methods: previous/next calendar-day controls, direct date picker, and bidirectional continuous/infinite scrolling across midnight with clear date separators. Today jumps to the current airport-local day/live position. Fetch windows incrementally, maintain scroll position and indicate unavailable historical/future coverage; never imply infinite data availability. Filter by arrival/departure and evidence class Published, Recorded, Inferred or All. A single movement may hold multiple kinds of evidence, not duplicate entries for published and observed records. Clearly distinguish sourced schedules, actual DOAA observations, and model-derived inferences. Every inference carries confidence, supporting evidence, method/provenance and contextual explanation, never masquerades as a confirmed movement. Timezones, date boundaries, deduplication and uncertain timings must be handled explicitly. The same rules and data power both presentations; implementation pending.

- **BOARD and Inspector Movements presentation (locked 2026-10-10).** Both are presentations of the same canonical movement records, filters, evidence taxonomy, and bidirectional time navigation. BOARD retains a dense, glanceable airport departure-board aesthetic with prominent local times, flight identifiers, routes, direction and statuses; Airfield > Movements presents the same information in a more spacious editorial/investigative Inspector layout. Shared model, not duplicated engines or contradictory records. Both preserve access to evidence details and Flight/Contact Inspector navigation, with appropriate responsive and accessible behavior. Design lock; not yet implemented.

- **BOARD default chronology (locked 2026-10-10).** BOARD opens to a unified, chronological feed of arrivals and departures together, not split panels. Arrival/departure filters remain available, alongside Published/Recorded/Inferred evidence filters. Time is the primary scan anchor; flight/route and operational state follow. Preserve bidirectional time browsing, airport-local date controls, provenance, and row navigation to Inspector. Same underlying movements as Airfield > Movements, in a denser airport-board presentation. Design decision only; not yet implemented.

- **BOARD row interaction (locked 2026-10-10).** Selecting any movement row opens the existing Inspector directly, with no intermediary inline expansion or competing details surface. Resolve the best grounded target: Flight for a real flight record (including matched published+observed movements), Contact for an observation-only encounter, and an explicitly provisional evidence/explanation view for inferred-only movements that lack a verified Flight or Contact. Never fabricate an object solely to satisfy navigation. Preserve BOARD scroll/date/filter position and Inspector back history on close/back. Inspector Explore links expose related Flight, Contact, Airframe, Journey and Airfield where evidenced. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Movement ordering and lifecycle (locked 2026-10-10).** BOARD and Airfield Movements use a stable scheduled-time anchor when a grounded published scheduled time exists. New estimates, observed phases and actual arrival/departure times update the same movement row in place without reordering it, while retaining evidence and timestamp history. For movements without published schedules, sort by the best supported observed event time, or explicitly labelled inferred time if no stronger anchor exists; later corrections remain explainable. Show estimated/actual times and major events (diversion, cancellation, go-around, relevant ground stop) prominently but without noisy row movement. Match and merge multi-source evidence into one movement only when defensible, preserving provenance. Design lock, implementation pending.

- **BOARD live-edge return affordance (locked 2026-10-10).** BOARD and Airfield Movements respect the user's current reading position while live movement data updates. When new or materially changed activity arrives at the live edge and the user is browsing elsewhere, show a compact floating directional arrow labelled Live / New activity, positioned without covering rows. Tapping jumps to the live edge and clears the pending-update indicator. Do not auto-scroll historical/date-selected browsing or close/change an Inspector selection; preserve scroll, date and filters when returning. When already following live, updates appear in place without forced jumps. Arrow direction follows actual timeline orientation. No indicator for mere polling with no meaningful new data. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Movement provenance pills (locked 2026-10-10).** Published, Recorded and Inferred are consistently styled provenance labels on movement rows in BOARD and Airfield Movements; a movement can display multiple pills because evidence types can coexist. Inferred is not automatically a warning/error state and should not be sensationalized with special alarming treatment. Each pill opens contextual evidence detail: source, observed or published timestamps, and, for inferred evidence, supporting observations, derivation method and confidence/uncertainty. Keep row glanceable; row selection itself opens the appropriate Inspector object. Provenance controls and row navigation must be separate accessible targets, without nested interactive elements. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 21 — tiered operational-event emphasis (locked 2026-10-10).** In BOARD and Airfield Movements, ordinary flight phases (taxiing, climbing, approaching, arrived) remain understated; significant delays use restrained caution emphasis; consequential confirmed disruptions such as cancellations or diversions receive stronger but non-alarmist prominence. Non-emergency aviation manoeuvres (holding, circling, go-arounds, touch-and-go) are interesting operational events, not inherently emergencies, and receive neutral treatment. Airport-wide ground stops belong in Airfield status and only affect individual movement rows where that linkage is evidenced. Operational state is separate from Published/Recorded/Inferred provenance and its confidence; event detail reveals timestamps, sources, supporting observations and uncertainty. Avoid over-colouring the board or overstating severity. Design lock, not implementation verification.

- **Decision 22 — Catchment Selector as shared airport context (approved design direction 2026-10-10).** BOARD must follow the existing Catchment Selector, not maintain its own independent remembered airport. Distinguish persistent **Home airfield** from the **currently explored catchment/airfield**: exploring elsewhere changes the active context used by SKY and BOARD without overwriting Home; setting Home changes the persistent default independently without unexpectedly switching the current exploration. Offer a clear Return Home action. Preserve the Catchment Selector's geographic coverage/radius semantics rather than reducing catchments to airport codes. Airfield Inspector may inspect related airports without silently mutating Home. Exact cross-view propagation and persistence are to be confirmed against existing selector implementation before coding. Design direction recorded; implementation pending.

- **Decision 22 refinement — inspection does not change catchment (locked 2026-10-10).** Opening an Airfield Inspector for an airport other than the active catchment does not silently change the Catchment Selector, SKY geographic focus, BOARD airport, or Home airfield. Provide an explicit **Explore this catchment** action on the Airfield Inspector; only that deliberate action switches the active exploration context, preserving Home. The distinction between inspecting an object and navigating the application's geographic context is universal. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 23 — return Home on launch (locked 2026-10-10).** On a fresh DOAA session, initialize the active exploration catchment from the persistent user-selected Home airfield/catchment, not the last explored airport. Exploring elsewhere changes only the current session's active context; recent explored catchments remain available for convenient return. Inspecting another airport changes neither Home nor active catchment. BOARD and SKY follow the active Catchment Selector. Preserve existing coverage/radius semantics. Distinguish fresh launch from in-session view switching so navigating between SKY, BOARD and Inspector does not reset exploration. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 24 — Contact Inspector tabs (locked 2026-10-10).** Contact Inspector uses Overview, Journey, and Track History. Overview remains the immediate SKY first-click view with identity, current flight phase, observation freshness, rich live telemetry, grounded route progress and compact punctuality summary where a Flight is linked, plus consistent EXPLORE links. Journey is the shared timestamped previous/current/next operational chronology also available from Flight, with clear source/uncertainty distinctions. Track History is the time-navigable telemetry and observed path within this specific Contact/encounter, including altitude/speed changes, detected manoeuvres, gaps and provenance; it must not be confused with Airframe Sightings, which enumerates separate DOAA encounters with a physical aircraft. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 25 — synchronized Track History map and charts (locked 2026-10-10).** Contact > Track History gives equal visual importance to the geographically grounded observed track map and synchronized altitude/speed telemetry charts. A shared time cursor/scrubber moves the aircraft position and chart cursors together; selecting a detected event navigates both to its observed timestamp/segment. Show heading, relevant telemetry, timestamps, observation freshness, source attribution and gaps without falsely interpolating missing tracks or presenting schematic curves as observed geometry. Responsive layout may place views side by side on wide screens and stacked on mobile, preserving synchronization and accessible keyboard/touch navigation. Detected phases/manoeuvres remain evidence-qualified, not claims about pilot intent. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 26 — Track History playback (locked 2026-10-10).** Contact Track History includes real time-based Play/Pause, timeline scrubber and playback-speed controls, synchronized with observed track map and telemetry charts. Replay respects actual timestamp intervals, marks missing observation gaps, and never invents continuous positions. Controls remain accessible; playback is paused/stopped when leaving context as appropriate. **Multi-aircraft comparison (promising direction, not yet locked):** an optional expanded Compare Tracks workspace may allow multiple Contacts/airframes to be replayed on a shared absolute clock with separate observed tracks and gaps, while ordinary Contact Track History stays single-target by default. Do not align tracks by percentage of flight completion or fabricate simultaneous encounters. Design lock for playback only; implementation pending.

- **Decision 27 — multi-aircraft Compare Tracks (locked 2026-10-10).** Multi-aircraft replay is an optional expanded workspace launched from Contact Track History, with Add to comparison available from related Contact exploration where appropriate. Compare Tracks prioritizes a large geographically grounded map with individually distinguishable observed tracks, a single absolute-time playback clock, scrubbing, speed controls, and visible observation gaps. **No per-aircraft telemetry charts or statistics in multi mode**: comparison is for spatial/temporal relationships, not a crowded analytical dashboard. Selecting an aircraft may open its full Inspector for detailed stats. Single-contact Track History retains synchronized map, charts, telemetry and playback. Never align tracks by flight-completion percentage or interpolate missing encounters. Design locked; implementation pending.

- **Decision 27 refinement — compact synchronized compare telemetry (user-directed 2026-10-10).** In Compare Tracks, retain the large shared replay map and playback controls, but show simple horizontally aligned **Track 1 / Track 2** readouts directly beneath playback, each with speed, altitude and heading or ground track at the same absolute timestamp. This supersedes the earlier shorthand 'no stats' for multi mode: no full charts or dense per-aircraft analytical dashboard, but essential side-by-side instantaneous telemetry is desired, particularly for nearby aircraft. Label heading versus ground track correctly according to sourced telemetry, show missing/stale values honestly, and avoid implied separation precision without synchronized reliable observations. For additional tracks, maintain legible comparable alignment. Visual refinement captured; implementation pending.

- **Decision 28 — Compare Tracks exactly two aircraft (locked 2026-10-10).** Compare Tracks supports a maximum of two simultaneously replayed aircraft, not expandable to three or four. Maintain a shared absolute-time map and playback controls with two horizontally aligned compact telemetry panels immediately beneath them (speed, altitude, sourced heading or ground track). The two-track limit preserves direct comparison and phone usability. Allow replacement/removal of either selected track without losing the other; unavailable observations are explicit. Design lock, implementation pending.

- **Decision 29 — Compare Tracks second-aircraft picker (locked 2026-10-10).** From Contact > Track History, **Compare with…** opens a deliberate Track 2 selector. Prioritize other actually observed Contacts with overlapping observation windows and geographic proximity, and provide callsign/registration search to find a specific recorded Contact. Never auto-select a comparison aircraft, infer an unobserved encounter, or imply missing history is available. After selection, open the two-aircraft Compare Tracks workspace with one absolute-time playback clock, grounded map tracks and aligned compact speed/altitude/heading-or-track telemetry. Allow replacement of Track 2. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 30 — full combined Compare Tracks playback window (locked 2026-10-10).** For two recorded aircraft, Compare Tracks uses the union of their observed time ranges rather than restricting playback to the overlap. The common absolute-time timeline distinguishes Track 1 coverage, Track 2 coverage, and overlapping periods; each aircraft marker and its instantaneous telemetry appear only where observation support is sufficient. Clearly show gaps, one-aircraft-only intervals and no-data periods, without manufacturing positions or proximity. Keep playback and scrubbing coherent across the whole combined range. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 31 — hybrid Track History / Compare Tracks camera (locked 2026-10-10).** Both single-contact Track History and two-aircraft Compare Tracks initially fit the complete geographically grounded observed tracks in a fixed overview, preserving spatial context during playback. Offer an optional explicit **Follow** control to keep the replaying aircraft in view; in two-track comparison, Follow must clearly identify its target and not imply both aircraft can always be framed tightly. Any manual pan/zoom disengages Follow immediately, preserving user control. Camera changes must not alter the shared playback clock or telemetry. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 32 — optional replay loop (locked 2026-10-10).** In both Contact Track History and two-aircraft Compare Tracks, playback stops at the final observed timestamp by default, retaining the final map state and timestamp-specific telemetry. Offer an explicit **Loop** toggle; when enabled, reaching the end restarts playback from the beginning of the selected replay window. Loop is off by default. Do not reset map camera or alter observed data when looping. Design lock; implementation pending.

- **Decision 33 — Airframe BIO hybrid dossier and life timeline (locked 2026-10-10).** Airframe > BIO begins with an editorial reference dossier of the particular physical airframe (manufacturer, model/variant, manufacturer serial number where evidenced, construction/first-flight/delivery dates, registrations, operators and configuration as available), followed by a clear visual chronological life history of sourced milestones, ownership/registration/operator changes and significant documented modifications. BIO is enduring physical-airframe identity and history; Operations is verified operational activity, Sightings is DOAA-observed encounters. Registration is not a permanent airframe identity; preserve physical continuity through reliable serial/identity evidence, dates, source provenance and explicit uncertainty. Do not turn BIO into a generic model-spec sheet or invent missing milestones. Design locked; implementation pending.

- **Decision 34 — curated, era-based Airframe BIO timeline (locked 2026-10-10).** BIO's chronological life history presents significant documented milestones (manufacture, first flight, delivery, registration/operator transitions, major modifications, retirement/preservation) rather than routine movements or DOAA encounters, which belong in Operations and Sightings. Group related events into meaningful dated eras (e.g. operator tenure), expandable to reveal individual evidenced milestones. Preserve source-level date precision (day/month/year/approximate range) and visibly qualify uncertainty or conflicting chronology; do not manufacture exact dates or implied continuity. Design locked; implementation pending.

- **Decision 35 — full Airframe BIO technical specifications (locked 2026-10-10).** BIO includes a comprehensive, category-organized technical reference, not merely a compact model summary. Clearly distinguish **this physical airframe's evidenced configuration** (installed engines, cabin/seating, modifications, equipment and other individual attributes where known) from **model/variant reference specifications** (dimensions, weights, capacities, performance, range, powerplant options, etc.). Permit full available sourced detail via expandable sections while retaining the editorial identity dossier and curated life timeline. Label representative/typical/range/option values explicitly; never present model specifications as confirmed installed or measured characteristics of an individual aircraft. Preserve provenance, units, applicability, date/version and unavailable states. Design locked; implementation pending.

- **Decision 36 — Airframe Operations reuses shared chronological timeline (locked 2026-10-10).** Airframe > Operations uses the same reusable bidirectional scrollable timeline mechanism and navigation grammar already specified for Airfield Movements / BOARD (date separators, airport-/context-appropriate date navigation, Today jump, stable ordering, explicit coverage gaps, filters and retained reading position), adapted to the identity of one physical airframe rather than one airport. Reuse actual component/state/navigation infrastructure where possible, with airframe-specific queries, provenance and row presentations; do not fork a visually similar but independently behaving implementation. An operational summary may precede the timeline (documented activity patterns, routes, notable events), but the timeline remains the main investigative surface. Preserve the distinction between independently documented operations and DOAA-recorded Sightings; do not imply an exhaustive flight history. Design locked; implementation pending.

- **Decision 37 — comprehensive Airframe Operations timeline (locked 2026-10-10).** Airframe > Operations displays every documented flight/movement associated with the evidenced physical airframe, not only curated notable events. Ordinary operations appear with restrained visual treatment; sourced unusual events, delays, diversions and other material changes receive proportionate emphasis and filtering. Preserve chronological context and stable ordering, deduplicate multiple claims about the same underlying operation without discarding their provenance, and distinguish recorded/documented coverage from unknown historical periods. 'Comprehensive' means exhaustive presentation of available documented records, **not** a claim that every flight the aircraft ever made is known. Design locked; implementation pending.
